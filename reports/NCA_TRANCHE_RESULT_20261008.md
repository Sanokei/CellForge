The founder was a valid numerical and prediction duplicate of the identity
reference. The mutation invocation timed out without a final response. This
outcome does not establish that the fixed architecture is incapable.

## Accounting and limitations

Two scientific CLI invocations consumed 2,976 literal positions per arm. Four
known non-scientific diagnostic CLI invocations are separate overhead. The second
random arm was charged 1,488 positions but was never computed: terminal HALT
prevents further evaluation. Thus the two-attempt numerical paired comparison is
incomplete; equal charged positions do not imply equal executed control work,
equal backend requests, equal latency or equal monetary expenditure.

The founder's complete observed event stream passed the zero-tool audit. The
mutation's incomplete stream cannot establish accepted zero-tool completion.
Tool prevention was never claimed; generic platform context and built-in skill
descriptions remained. Backend request counts and monetary costs are unknown.
The reported subscription usage stayed at 15%; it is too coarse to infer cost.

The founder packet's optimization objective was implicit and its prohibition of
“fitting” was not qualified as gradient fitting. This prospective design weakness
was noticed after the founder result and recorded without altering the frozen
mutation packet. It is not a demonstrated cause of either observed outcome.
